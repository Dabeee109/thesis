🔒 Legal & Data Privacy Compliance

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Statement

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Governing Law: Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012) • Meta Platform Policy Compliant

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Diner's Cafe Ordering & Smart Delivery System ("we", "our", or "us"), located in Tondo, Manila, Philippines. We operate an AI-powered conversational food ordering service through Facebook Messenger, our web portals, and mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, use, store, and protect your personal information when you interact with our Facebook Messenger Chatbot, place food and beverage orders, request delivery, or access our online services.

By interacting with our Facebook Messenger AI Bot, submitting an order, or providing customer information, you signify that you have read, understood, and consented to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy pursuant to Republic Act No. 10173.

2. Information We Collect

To process and deliver your orders accurately, we collect the following categories of data:

A. Meta Messenger Platform Data

  • Page-Scoped ID (PSID): A unique, pseudonymous identifier assigned by Meta to your chat session with our Facebook Page.
  • Public Messenger Profile Name: Your first and last name as authorized through your Meta Messenger profile to address you politely.

B. Order Fulfillment & Delivery Information

  • Customer Name & Contact Number: Active Philippine phone number (e.g., 09XXXXXXXXX) required for dispatch and rider communication.
  • Delivery Address & Landmarks: Street address, barangay, city, and landmarks to guide delivery personnel.
  • Geographic Coordinates / Location Pins: Latitude, longitude, or Google Maps link shared voluntarily to calculate delivery distance and fares within our 3.0 km service zone.
  • Dining Preferences & Special Notes: Item customizations, sweetness level, add-on preferences, and special preparation instructions.

C. Payment & Discount Verification Data

  • Payment Method: Selection of Cash on Delivery/Pick-up, GCash, or Xendit.
  • GCash Transaction Proof: Customer-submitted GCash reference number, transaction amount, and screenshot of payment confirmation used exclusively for payment verification.
  • Senior Citizen / PWD Identification: Valid ID number or image submitted voluntarily to avail of statutory 20% discounts under Philippine law.

3. How We Use Your Information (Purpose of Processing)

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes, including:

  • Order Fulfillment: Taking, processing, kitchen cooking, and packing your food and drink orders.
  • Smart Delivery Routing: Verifying delivery feasibility within our 3.0 km store radius, computing fair distance-based delivery fees, and guiding riders to your doorstep.
  • Payment Verification & Auditing: Matching GCash reference numbers and amounts against merchant records to detect discrepancies and prevent transaction errors.
  • Real-Time Order Tracking & Notifications: Pushing updates on order statuses (Pending Approval, Preparing, Dispatched, Delivered) via Messenger.
  • Customer Support & Human Handover: Facilitating live handover to store staff when order modifications or assistance are needed.

4. Conversational AI & Third-Party Service Integrations

Our ordering system utilizes trusted third-party technology providers under strict security guidelines:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (Messenger API): Powers the messaging gateway. Meta processes data in accordance with their Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini AI (LLM Service): Interprets conversational food requests, recommendations, and intent parsing. Customer messages are transmitted via encrypted API exclusively for processing conversational intent and are not used to train public models.
  • OpenStreetMap / Google Maps Platform: Used solely for geocoding addresses and calculating route distances.
  • GCash (G-Xchange, Inc.) & Xendit: Used for processing digital payments. We do not store your MPIN, bank credentials, or financial account passwords.

5. Data Security, Storage & Retention

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012, Diner's Cafe implements organizational, physical, and technical measures to safeguard your personal data:

  • Access Control: Administrative dashboards and staff portals are password-protected with role-based access control (Admin, Cashier, Kitchen, Rider).
  • Encrypted Communications: All web traffic and webhook communications use HTTPS / TLS encryption and HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.
  • Retention Policy: Temporary chat sessions, uncommitted carts, and draft details are automatically purged after 30 minutes of inactivity. Completed transaction records are archived securely for accounting and legal auditing purposes, after which they are safely anonymized or deleted.

6. Your Rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

As a data subject, you are entitled to the following statutory rights:

  • Right to be Informed: To know whether personal data pertaining to you is being processed.
  • Right to Access: To request a summary of personal information we maintain regarding your profile or orders.
  • Right to Rectification: To correct inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete details in your delivery profile.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: To suspend, withdraw, or order the removal of your personal information from our system.
  • Right to Damages: To be indemnified for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, or unlawful processing of your data.

7. User Data Deletion Instructions (Meta Compliance)

In accordance with Meta Platform Rules for Facebook Login and Messenger Chatbots, you have the right to request deletion of your data at any time:

  • Instant Chat Reset: Send the keyword Reset or Clear cart directly in our Facebook Messenger chat to immediately wipe active draft items, temporary notes, and cached session information.
  • Meta Platform Removal: Go to your Facebook profile Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites, locate Diner's Cafe, and click Remove.
  • Formal Data Deletion Request: If you wish to permanently delete all archived customer records, phone numbers, and past order history associated with your Facebook PSID, send an email to dinerscafemanila@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request - [Your Name]". Our Data Protection team will process your request within three (3) business days.
