🗑️ Meta Platform Data Deletion Compliance

User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with the Meta Platform Developer Policy and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), Diner's Cafe respects your right to have your personal data, chat history, and order profile deleted upon request.

Method 1: Instant Chat & Cart Reset (Immediate)

If you wish to immediately erase your active session, uncommitted cart, temporary notes, and chat context from our system:

  • Open our chat on Facebook Messenger (search for Diner's Cafe).
  • Type and send the command Reset or Clear cart.
  • Our chatbot will instantly delete your cached draft items, delivery coordinates, active conversation thread, and reset your state back to IDLE.

Method 2: Remove Diner's Cafe via Facebook Settings

You can revoke Messenger permissions and remove Diner's Cafe from your Meta account directly:

  • Open Facebook on your mobile device or desktop browser.
  • Navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  • Scroll down to the Permissions section and click Apps and Websites.
  • Locate Diner's Cafe in the list of active apps and click Remove.
  • Confirm the prompt. Meta will immediately cease passing your profile information to our platform.

Method 3: Permanent Database Erasure Request

If you wish to have all your historical database records (including past order receipts, archived phone numbers, delivery addresses, and PSID associations) completely and permanently purged from our database:

📧 Send an email to: dinerscafemanila@gmail.com
Subject: Data Deletion Request - [Your Full Name]

Please include the following details in your message:

  • Your Full Name as it appears on your Facebook profile.
  • The contact phone number used for delivery (if applicable).
  • Approximate date of your recent order or interaction.

Our Data Protection Officer will review and permanently wipe all matching records from our primary database within three (3) business days, and issue an official Confirmation of Data Erasure to your email.
